# DuelRush — English Duels

## App name

DuelRush

## Short description

Learn English through smart practice, listening, and live language duels.

## Full description

Turn English practice into a challenge you will want to return to.

DuelRush combines focused lessons with fast language duels. Train at your own pace, challenge a friend, or find an opponent online and put your knowledge into practice.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Train at Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels
• Practise grammar, vocabulary, sentence building, and listening
• Improve pronunciation with voice exercises
• Play live English duels and send challenges to friends
• Keep learning offline in practice matches
• Review duel results and understand your mistakes
• Save useful words in your personal vocabulary
• Build streaks, unlock achievements, and follow your progress
• Compare results on the leaderboard
• Discover a new English tip every day

PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE

Every session contains a clear set of questions. Helpful hints and answer explanations make it easier to understand why an answer is correct instead of simply memorising it.

DUELS THAT MATCH YOUR PACE

Choose a difficulty level and compete in a short English challenge. You can find an online opponent, invite a friend, or practise without waiting. After a duel, review the score and the answers that need more attention.

YOUR LEARNING SPACE

Keep useful words, track achievements, customise your profile, and see how your skills develop over time. Privacy and safety controls let you remove friends, block users, report inappropriate behaviour, and delete your account.

Microphone access is used only when you start a pronunciation exercise. Core learning features remain available without microphone permission.

Ready for your next English challenge? Start your DuelRush.
